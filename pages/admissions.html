Application Journey

Our selection process is rigorous, ensuring only the most dedicated students enter our workshops.

Step 1: Online Application

Submit your personal details, academic history, and a letter of motivation.

Step 2: Technical Assessment

Complete a fine-motor skill test and basic mechanical aptitude evaluation.

Step 3: Personal Interview

Meet with our head instructors to discuss your passion for horology.

Step 4: Enrollment Offer

Receive your formal acceptance and secure your seat with a deposit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior engineering experience?

No prior engineering experience is required for the foundation program. However, a high degree of patience, steady hands, and attention to detail are essential.

Is there an age limit for students?

We accept students from age 18. There is no upper age limit, provided the candidate passes the physical dexterity assessments.

Are the classes taught in English?

Yes, all our international programs are conducted in English. We also offer specialized workshops in French and German.
