The Beginning

Born in Geneva, Refined by Time

The Horology Academy was founded in 1998 by Marcus Van Der Rohe, a master watchmaker with a vision to preserve the endangered skills of mechanical restoration. What started as a small workshop in the heart of Geneva has grown into a world-renowned institution.

Today, we stand as a bridge between the historical techniques of the 18th century and the cutting-edge micro-engineering of the modern era. Our graduates are the keepers of time, ensuring that the world's most complex mechanical hearts continue to beat with precision.

Strategic Foundations

Corporate Purpose & Pillars of Excellence

HorLux stands as an institution built upon uncompromising values, Swiss engineering rigor, and the preservation of an immortalized luxury craft. Our operations are governed by five strategic mandates.

01

Our Core Vision

To serve as the definitive global benchmark for horological education, setting an elite standard where historical watchmaking craftsmanship and next-generation micro-engineering coexist in perfect perpetuity.

  • Establishing universal academic standards in luxury horology
  • Blending 18th-century finishing techniques with 21st-century micro-mechanics
  • Driving advanced mechanical innovation through dedicated horological labs
02

Educational Mission

Providing rigorous, WOSTEP-equivalent training programs that arm prospective master watchmakers with world-class spatial dexterity, micro-assembly skills, and uncompromising precision.

03

Heritage Conservation

Preserving the endangered secrets of mechanical complication repair, finishing techniques, and micro-component restoration to keep the historic art form alive.

04

Technological Rigor

Housing state-of-the-art cleanroom workshops equipped with elite Swiss machinery, ensuring students gain fluency in both manual craft and automated production techniques.

05

Industry Integration

Maintaining active corporate pipelines with elite Swiss manufactures to facilitate direct post-graduate placement, driving global economic credibility for the Academy.
