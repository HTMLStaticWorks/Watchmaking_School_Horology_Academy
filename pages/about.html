The Beginning

Born in Geneva, Refined by Time

The Horology Academy was founded in 1998 by Marcus Van Der Rohe, a master watchmaker with a vision to preserve the endangered skills of mechanical restoration. What started as a small workshop in the heart of Geneva has grown into a world-renowned institution.

Today, we stand as a bridge between the historical techniques of the 18th century and the cutting-edge micro-engineering of the modern era. Our graduates are the keepers of time, ensuring that the world's most complex mechanical hearts continue to beat.

"We don't just teach people how to fix watches; we teach them how to understand the soul of mechanical time."
