Since 1998

Legacy of Swiss Precision

Founded on the principles of mechanical perfection, the Horology Academy has been at the forefront of watchmaking education for over two decades. Our mission is to preserve the ancient art of horology while embracing modern engineering innovations.

We provide students with the environment, tools, and mentorship needed to master complex movements and restore the world's most valuable timepieces.

"Watchmaking is not just a profession; it's a lifelong pursuit of perfection."

- Marcus Van Der Rohe, Founder

Academy Workshops

The Master's Workbench

Experience the precision of high-end horology in our state-of-the-art workshops. Our academy provides students with authentic Swiss-made tools and vintage restoration equipment.

From simple quartz maintenance to complex tourbillon assembly, we cover every aspect of the craft with hands-on mentorship.
